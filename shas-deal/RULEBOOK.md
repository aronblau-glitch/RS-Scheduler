# SHAS DEAL: Rulebook and Card List

A fast card game of collecting, trading and out-arguing your opponents. Inspired by Shasopoly and built in the style of Monopoly Deal, with some new twists.

- **Players:** 2-5
- **Time:** 15-25 minutes
- **Ages:** 10+
- **Components:** 106 main-deck cards, 10 Daily Page cards, 8 Secret Goal cards

---

## 1. Goal

Be the first player to have **3 complete property sets of different colors** on the table. A Secret Goal can count as a half-set (see 7.6).

---

## 2. Setup

1. Shuffle the 106-card main deck. Deal **5 cards** to each player.
2. Shuffle the Daily Page deck and the Secret Goal deck. Place them face down.
3. Deal each player **1 Secret Goal**. Keep it hidden until you complete it.
4. The youngest player goes first.

Each player has three areas:
- **Hand:** private.
- **Bank:** face-up money pile. Cards in the bank are always used as money.
- **Table:** your properties, grouped by set.

---

## 3. Turn Structure

1. **Flip.** At the start of each *round* (when play returns to the first player), flip a Daily Page card. Its effect lasts until the next flip.
2. **Draw 2 cards.** If your hand is empty, draw 5 instead.
3. **Play up to 3 cards.** Each card can be:
   - put in your **bank** (money and action cards only),
   - played to your **table** (properties),
   - or played as an **action**.
4. **Discard down to 7 cards** at the end of your turn.

You may play fewer than 3 cards. Cards played from a hand cannot be taken back.

---

## 4. Paying

When you owe money:
- Pay from your **bank** and/or from **properties on your table**.
- There is **no change**. If you overpay, you lose the difference.
- If you can't cover the full amount, you give everything you have.
- You cannot pay with cards from your hand.
- Action cards in your bank are worth the number printed on them. Property cards are worth their printed value.
- The receiver puts money in their bank and properties on their table.

---

## 5. Property Sets

A set is complete when you hold the full number of cards for that color.

| Color | Set name | Size | Rent (1 / 2 / 3 / 4 cards) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | Zeraim | 3 | 1 / 2 / 4 / - |
| Blue | Moed | 4 | 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 |
| Pink | Nashim | 3 | 1 / 2 / 4 / - |
| Orange | Nezikin | 4 | 1 / 3 / 4 / 6 |
| Red | Kodashim | 3 | 2 / 3 / 5 / - |
| Purple | Tohorot | 3 | 1 / 3 / 5 / - |
| Gold | Commentators | 2 | 2 / 3 / - / - |
| Grey | Study Tools | 2 | 1 / 2 / - / - |

Each rent value is also the number of coins owed.

- Properties of the same color go in one set.
- A **wild property** can be moved between its colors at the start of your turn, and this does not count as a card play.
- You may hold more than one complete set of the same color, but only different colors count toward winning.

---

## 6. Action Cards

Actions go in front of you when played, then into the discard pile. Targeted actions give the target a chance to respond with a **Machloket** card (see 7.2).

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Deal Breaker** | Steal a **complete** set from any player. |
| **Sly Deal** | Steal 1 property from any player. It cannot come from a complete set. |
| **Forced Deal** | Swap 1 of your properties with 1 of another player's. It cannot come from a complete set. |
| **Debt Collector** | One player pays you 5. |
| **Celebration** | Every player pays you 2. |
| **Machloket** | Blocks an action played against you (see 7.2). |
| **Draw Two** | Draw 2 extra cards. |
| **Double the Rent** | Play together with a Rent card. Doubles the rent. This uses 2 of your 3 plays. |
| **Study Hall** | Add to a **complete** set. That set's rent is +3. One per set. |
| **Library** | Add to a complete set that already has a Study Hall. That set's rent is +4 more. One per set. |

**Rent cards:**
- A two-color Rent card charges **one player** for rent on one of its colors that you own.
- A Wild Rent card charges **all players** for rent on any one color you own.
- Rent amount = the value in the set table for the number of cards in that set, plus any Study Hall, Library and Commentary bonuses.

---

## 7. Twists

### 7.1 Daily Page
At the start of each round, flip a Daily Page card. Its effect applies to everyone until the next flip. See the Daily Page list in section 10.

### 7.2 Machloket (debate)
A **Machloket** card cancels an action played against you. It works like "Just Say No" in other games, with one difference: the attacker can **challenge** it.

1. The defender plays Machloket. The action is blocked.
2. The attacker may challenge by revealing the top card of the draw pile. The defender does the same.
3. The higher **money value** wins. Properties use their printed value, and action cards count as 0.
4. If the attacker wins, the action goes through. If the defender wins, the block stands. A tie repeats the reveal.
5. A challenge can repeat at most **3 times** per action. After the 3rd reveal, the block stands.
6. Revealed cards go to the discard pile.

### 7.3 Chavruta (study partner)
Play a **Chavruta** card on another player. They may accept or decline. If they accept:
- Choose **one set each** to share. Rent on both sets is split between you and your partner, and each of you can add cards to both.
- If either shared set becomes complete, **both** of you count it toward your win.
- Either partner can end the pairing on their own turn. Shared cards stay with whoever played them.

### 7.4 Wild Commentary
Play it on any set (complete or not). That set gets **+1 rent**. A player who plays a Deal Breaker on that set must discard **1 card from their hand per Commentary** on it, and the Commentary is discarded.

### 7.5 Market Swing
Every player passes **1 banked card** (their choice) to the player on their left. This helps the player who is behind catch up.

### 7.6 Secret Goal
Each player holds one hidden Secret Goal. When you complete it, reveal it. It counts as **half a set** toward your win, so two Secret Goals can't win the game, but a goal plus 2.5 sets can. See section 10 for the list.

### 7.7 Comeback Bonus
If you have **2 or more fewer complete sets** than the leader at the start of your turn, you draw **1 extra card**.

### 7.8 Pop Quiz
Play it on any player. They answer a question the group chooses (agree on a topic before the game, such as trivia, study material or general knowledge).
- **Correct:** the quizzer steals one property of the answerer's choice. It can't come from a complete set.
- **Wrong:** the answerer pays the quizzer 3.
- The Pop Quiz can't be blocked by Machloket.

---

## 8. Winning

The game ends **immediately** when a player has 3 complete sets of different colors (a Secret Goal counts as half a set). If the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile.

**Tiebreaker:** if two players complete their 3rd set at the same time through a Chavruta, they win together.

## 10. Side Decks

### Daily Page deck (10 cards)
1. **Double Rent Day:** all rent is doubled.
2. **Open Market:** all wild properties count as any color.
3. **Frozen Bank:** banked cards can't be used to pay this round (properties only).
4. **Generous Day:** whenever you pay rent, you may pay 1 less.
5. **Quiet Round:** nobody can play Pop Quiz.
6. **Extra Hand:** everyone draws 3 cards at the start of their turn.
7. **Tight Hands:** hand limit is 5 this round.
8. **Open Hands:** all players play with their hands face up.
9. **Rest Day:** no rent can be charged this round.
10. **Trade Day:** Forced Deal doesn't need a target. You may swap with anyone.

### Secret Goal deck (8 cards)
1. Complete a set using at least 1 wild.
2. Have 3 different sets in progress at the same time.
3. Hold 10 or more in your bank at the start of your turn.
4. Win a Machloket challenge.
5. Collect rent of 6 or more in a single action.
6. Own a set with both a Study Hall and a Library.
7. Complete a set that includes a Wild Commentary.
8. Complete a Chavruta set with a partner.

---

## 11. Quick Rules Summary

- Draw 2, play up to 3, discard down to 7.
- Complete 3 different-color sets to win.
- Machloket blocks actions, but the attacker can challenge with deck reveals.
- Flip a Daily Page each round.
- Trailing by 2+ sets earns an extra draw.

---

## 12. Design Notes and Open Items

- Property names are placeholders and can be swapped for any theme without changing the rules.
- Rent values and card counts are a first pass. A few playtests will show whether Deal Breaker and Machloket are too strong or too weak.
- Pop Quiz topics and the Daily Page list are meant to be customized.
- Next steps: playtest with 2 and 4 players, tune Wild Rent and Deal Breaker counts, then design the card art.
