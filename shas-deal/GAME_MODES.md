# Two Ways to Play: Chavrusa Party and No Mercy

Two optional game modes for BavliDeal (and for the Shas Deal draft). Pick one before the game starts. Card names below use BavliDeal terms: Draw Two, Take One Property, Forced Exchange, Cancel, Collect a Debt, Siyum Collection, Bench, Study Hall and Take a Full Set.

Both modes use the standard 106-card deck. A few rules use the optional Twist Pack (Daily Page and Secret Goal cards). Where that is true, it says so.

| | Chavrusa Party | No Mercy |
|---|---|---|
| **Feel** | Loud, friendly, a little silly | Tense, competitive, nobody is safe |
| **Best for** | Family, mixed ages, classrooms, first-time players | Experienced players who want a harder game |
| **Players** | 2-6 (teams of 2 for 4 or 6) | 2-5 |
| **Length** | 20-30 minutes | 20-35 minutes |
| **Win** | 3 different full sets (teams: 4 combined) | 3 different full sets |

---

## Mode 1: Chavrusa Party (the fun way)

The goal of this mode is that everyone laughs, talks and learns the card titles as they play. Players are rewarded for style, not just strategy.

### Setup
Standard setup, plus:
- Shuffle the Daily Page deck (if you have it) and place it face down.
- Place a bowl or pile of spare cards as the **Party Pot** (see below).

### Rule 1: Say it aloud
When you play an Action card, read its title and meaning, then the effect. For example: "Ta shema, come and hear, I draw two."
- If you say all three parts, you draw **1 bonus card** (once per turn).
- If a table-mate helps you pronounce or recall it, you both draw 1.
- Anyone can look at the glossary pages if unsure. No penalty for asking.

### Rule 2: Dramatic Cancel
When you play a Cancel, you must give a one-sentence reason in character ("Meitivi! I object, that's not how the Mishnah reads!"). The reason is flavor only and doesn't change the result. The player who gets the biggest laugh (table vote) takes the top card of the discard pile.

### Rule 3: The Party Pot
Whenever a Siyum Collection is played, every player who pays adds **1 zuz** from their bank to the Party Pot instead of giving it to the player. The next player to complete a set takes the whole pot. This keeps losing players interested.

### Rule 4: Daily Page every round
Flip a Daily Page at the start of every round. Use only the lighter pages:
- Extra Draw
- Open Hands (everyone plays with hands face up, great for table talk)
- Generous Hand
- Quiet Market
- Open Market

Remove Double Rent, Sealed Purse and Teiku from the deck in this mode.

### Rule 5: Team play (4 or 6 players)
Split into teams of 2, sitting alternately.
- Teams win by completing **4 different sets combined** (each set belongs to one partner's table).
- Partners may talk openly about strategy, even in front of opponents.
- Partners may give each other cards from their hands once per turn (counts as a play).
- A player can pay a debt using their partner's bank, but only if the partner agrees.

### Rule 6: Handicaps
- **Younger or newer players:** draw 3 cards each turn instead of 2.
- **Experienced players:** play only 2 cards per turn.
- Handicaps are announced before the game, not mid-game.

### Rule 7: The Siyum finish
When a player or team wins, everyone says "Hadran alach" together. The winner also reads aloud one glossary term of their choice for the table to repeat. It's a nice way to end a loud game on a calm note.

### Fun mode quick reference
1. Say the title, meaning and effect: draw 1 bonus.
2. Give a funny reason when you Cancel.
3. Siyum payments go into the Party Pot.
4. Light Daily Pages every round.
5. Teams share advice and win with 4 combined sets.

## Mode 2: No Mercy (the hard way)

Nothing is safe, every payment hurts, and a lead is a target. Pick this mode only if everyone agrees.

### Setup
Standard setup, with these changes:
- **Remove 2 of the 3 Cancel cards** from the deck. Cancel is now rare.
- Remove the Comeback Bonus if you are using the Twist Pack.
- Hand limit is **5** instead of 7.

### Rule 1: No sanctuary
**Take One Property** and **Forced Exchange** can take cards from complete sets. Only Take a Full Set protects against being stripped one card at a time, because it takes the whole set intact. A broken set loses its Bench or Study Hall upgrades (discard them).

### Rule 2: Stack the debt
When someone plays **Collect a Debt** or **Siyum Collection** against you, you may play one of your own (same card type) to pass the charge to another opponent.
- The amount goes up by **+2 zuz** for each pass (Collect a Debt: 5, then 7, then 9; Siyum Collection: 2, then 4, then 6).
- The original player is skipped when passing, and the last player who cannot or will not pass pays.
- Cancel can be played at any point in the chain by the current target.

### Rule 3: Rent that stacks
Tartei (Double the Rent) can stack: you may play **two** on one rent for ×4 (this uses 3 of your plays). Study Hall and Bench count before multiplying.

### Rule 4: Foreclosure
If your bank and properties cannot cover a payment, you must also **discard cards from your hand** to cover the rest, using each card's corner value. If you still cannot pay, you pay what you can and your debt is cleared.

### Rule 5: The leader pays more
The player (or team) with the most complete sets, with ties counted for everyone, is the **leader**. The leader pays **+1 zuz** on every payment (rent, debts and siyum). When the gap is cleared, the extra charge ends.

### Rule 6: No passing
You must play at least 1 card each turn. If you have no legal play, discard 1 card instead. Skipping all plays isn't allowed.

### Rule 7: Harsh Daily Pages (Twist Pack, optional)
If you use the Daily Page deck, replace it with these 8 pages:

| Page | Effect |
|---|---|
| Double Rent | All rent is ×2. |
| Sealed Purse | Payments come from properties first, then the bank. |
| Teiku | Cancel can't be played this round. |
| Trade Day | Forced Exchange can target any card, even in complete sets. |
| Tight Hands | Hand limit is 3 this round. |
| The Crash | Every player discards half their bank, rounded up. |
| Hot Market | Every card played as money counts as 1 zuz less this round. |
| No Mercy Double | The leader pays +2 instead of +1 this round. |

### No Mercy quick reference
1. Cancel is rare (1 in the deck).
2. Complete sets can be picked apart.
3. Debts can be passed along, getting bigger each time.
4. Tartei can stack to ×4.
5. Hand cards cover shortfalls.
6. The leader always pays +1.
7. You must play at least 1 card.

## Balance notes and playtest questions

- **Fun mode:** Is the bonus draw for saying a title too strong? If so, make it +1 zuz in the bank instead.
- **Fun mode:** Is 4 combined sets right for teams of 2? Try 3 for a faster game.
- **No Mercy:** Debt passing may create endless chains in 5-player games. A cap of 3 passes is recommended.
- **No Mercy:** Foreclosure plus a 5-card hand limit may be too harsh. If so, raise the limit to 7.
- **No Mercy:** The leader tax can make the lead feel like a punishment. Test with +1 vs a half-set gap.
- Both modes should be tested at 2, 3 and 5 players.

## Mixing the modes
- Fun mode can be softened further by removing Collect a Debt and Siyum Collection entirely.
- No Mercy can be mixed with team play, but don't add Rule 7 (Siyum finish) sharing of cards.
- Don't mix Fun and No Mercy rules in the same game. The pacing doesn't hold up.
